## Motion contract

`compileNamedPullBy` owns four stable actor identities, current partner/neighbor maps and epoch, one complete named relationship, explicit left/right hand, exact two/four source counts, and stationary endpoint constraints. Participating actors physically exchange the named receiver's exact entry position. Nonparticipating actors retain their root slots. No proximity lookup, extra turn, synthetic time, relation rebind or unrelated figure alias is used.

The path bows each actor away from the passing hand and witnesses a true longitudinal crossing at half time. Both named hands meet at that gate. The source adapter derives only the supported following figure's entry facing. Its first and last poses match the declared incoming and outgoing poses. The ordinary following swing keeps its existing acquisition inside its own twelve counts: its named partners move inward immediately, reach the 0.62 m paired spacing after 1.68 counts, and acquire all three full-strength frame edges by 2.4 counts. The held middle then rotates normally. This is a bounded schematic connecting step, not a claim of polished or rapid acquisition; no musical counts are added.

The isolated primitive supports either named hand and both count budgets. Whole-source admission is narrower: four-count larks → partner swing and two-count partner → eight-count larks right allemande 1½, with exact standard longways corners. Other continuations fail closed. This is root/contact geometry, not an anatomical or dance-teaching safety certificate. Two-count facing changes remain brisk.
