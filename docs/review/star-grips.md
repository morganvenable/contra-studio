# Explicit star grips

## Scope

The registered star contact adapter distinguishes two explicit source contracts:

- Hands across: opposite dancers join the named hand, producing two independent pairs.
- Wrist grip: each dancer's named hand reaches the next dancer's distal forearm/wrist, producing a directed four-person cycle. Left and right stars use opposite cycle directions.

The requested grip changes neither root trajectories nor timing, turn amount, persistent identities or progression. An unspecified existing star retains its previous contact behavior. Unknown grips and grip arguments on unrelated figures are rejected.

The contact kind `wrist` occupies only the reaching hand. The recipient retains their own hand's outgoing connection. The renderer's wrist-only elbow construction keeps the recipient's forearm near the incoming palm without stretching either arm.

## Reviewed source

ContraDB 528, **Al's Safeway Produce**, Robert Cromartie, explicitly requests wrist-grip left and right stars. Its source note also requests the left-star-to-left-allemande wrist connection. The original source, unequal 6/10-count circle/swing timing, notes and reviewed note classification remain archived. A new instruction appended to the note, a changed turn direction, a different grip, or a disabled contact capability blocks that interpretation.

Definitions consulted include the Caller’s Box glossary, ContraLab definitions and Paul Balliet's glossary. Public readability of choreography is not a redistribution license.

## Evidence

- Unit tests distinguish the two graphs, both chiralities, sparse actor identities and rigid transforms.
- Every sampled root and facing is identical to the prior star over a full six-couple finite circulation.
- The actual fixed-length Avatar test checks 3,208 strong contacts; reaching wrists meet their targets within floating-point precision. The incoming palm is within 28.1 mm of the next actor's modeled distal-forearm segment.
- Independent actual-source/archive/chirality/full-period review passes five groups, including 201,344 contact checks across all 2–12-couple sizes.
- Independent twelve-Avatar mesh sampling over both stars at 50 samples per beat found positive other-person margins: unrelated arms 15.1 mm, other torso 320 mm, legs 106 mm, other head 235 mm. The existing free-hand/self-shirt graze is about 7.4 mm.

These are sampled procedural-geometry results, not continuous collision certificates, measured shared forces, or a claim of anatomically perfect wrist styling. Existing star gait limitations remain separate from contact semantics.
