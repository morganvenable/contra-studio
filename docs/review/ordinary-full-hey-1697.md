# Ordinary full hey: source-bound terminal composition

The new isolated `ordinary-full-hey.ts` adapter accepts sixteen source counts,
explicit paired current-neighbor sidelines, the first role, opposite center/end
shoulders, and the terminal `progressed-neighbors` exit. It does not implement a
full hey by composing two half-hey balance or swing exits. All eight complete
pass layers belong to one continuous trajectory; all twelve pair encounters
remain free of hand contacts.

## Source and independent identity oracle

The retained acceptance source is [Hey Fever, ContraDB 1697](https://contradb.com/dances/1697)
by Tony Parkes. Its six original source rows are neighbor balance and swing 16,
larks allemande left 1½ 8, partner swing 8, partner promenade 8, robin chain 8, and
robin-start full hey 16 with right shoulders in the center, left shoulders on the
ends, and a progression glyph. The source has no hook or choreographic note.
The omitted internal balance timing, promenade details, chain receiver and hey
side-pair/exit interpretation must remain visible review choices.

The independent corner oracle, ordered NW, NE, SE, SW with L1=0, R1=1, L2=2,
R2=3, is:

- start:1,0,3,2
- neighbor swing 16:2,3,0,1
- larks allemande 24 and partner swing 32:0,3,2,1
- partner promenade 40:2,1,0,3
- robin chain 48:2,3,0,1
- complete full hey 64:2,3,0,1

Thus the promenade and current-neighbor chain already place persistent couples
in their progressed rows. The full hey must preserve those exact identities,
open the compact sideline spacing to 1.8 model units, and face the next neighbor.
It never inserts another station permutation, neighbor reassignment or travel.
The enclosing finite-line compiler independently validates the complete actual
progression before repeat playback is allowed.

The explicit twelve-encounter order is:
WR(1,3); PL(2,3)(1,0); MR(2,0); NL(3,0)(2,1);
WR(3,1); PL(0,1)(3,2); MR(0,2); NL(1,2)(0,3).
