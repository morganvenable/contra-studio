# Reel Time: source-bound counterclockwise Becket star progression

## Scope and status

This is an isolated staged composition adapter, not a claim of universal reverse-Becket support. It has no title-based dispatch. The retained acceptance source is ContraDB [2446, Reel Time by Cary Ravitz](https://contradb.com/dances/2446), explicitly labeled “Becket ccw.” Its source publication label is **shared to sketchbook**. Public readability is not a redistribution license; the exact test snapshot retains the original label and reference link.

The adapter is registered by default through `becketStarProgressions` after independent actual-source/archive, complete finite-period, body and default UI checks. Explicitly disabling that capability still rejects this route. Diagonal heys, arbitrary Becket directions, double progression, and Susan Petrick's Northeast on Southwest are not covered by this route.

## Literal source and independent oracle

The source's seven rows occupy exactly 64 counts:

- A1: long lines forward/back 8; larks allemande left 1½ 8
- A2: neighbor balance and swing 16, explicitly reviewed as 4 + 12
- B1: star left once 8, source progression marker; next-neighbor do-si-do once 8
- B2: circle left ¾ 8; partner swing 8

The ContraDB source route retains the exact CCW formation and star marker. Its explicit review recipe is a four-count balance allocation on source row 2 plus an event policy selecting lark-left waiting, an eight-count same-end exchange and zero pre-event stance-preparation counts. The bare source leaves that omitted finite-end choice unresolved; the original source and selected review choices remain independently retained in its portable interpretation archive.

The source establishes CCW circulation. It does not establish its finite-end choreography. The adapter requires a separate retained `endPolicy`, with reviewed-interpretation evidence bound to the same source: lark-left waiting, eight-count exchange across the same physical end, zero re-entry preparation counts. Removing the policy, changing its role arrangement, or claiming it is source-explicit blocks compilation.

Independent four-corner order (west-top, east-top, east-bottom, west-bottom) for the first two couples:

- Start and after long lines: `[2,1,0,3]`
- After larks allemande: `[0,1,2,3]`
- After neighbor swing and full star: `[0,1,2,3]`

Thus east-side partners 0/1 physically finish in the upper row and west-side partners 2/3 in the lower row. This proves CCW circulation without reflecting a clockwise movie or exchanging role labels. The lark allemande and neighbor swing physically produce the row permutation. The complete left star winds once and returns to the same actual individual stations; only then, at beat 40, does the explicit source event bind the next neighbors.

The public immutable dancer-direction metadata names actual initial CCW travel (east/up, west/down); role identity and lark-left placement remain independent. Each round uses its explicit current slot directions rather than that initial metadata.

The event retains original actor and partner IDs. Current neighbors are explicit reciprocal identities in carried groups, never guessed by proximity. In particular, two couples can both be out; the next round's opening half remains inactive rather than reconstructing an active group merely because the end pairs now face inward.

## Physical ends and repeatability

Each newly inactive couple occupies a real top or bottom endpoint. Partners exchange across that same endpoint during beats 40–48, turn inward with lark-left placement, and hold still until the next source-bound neighbor change at beat 40 of the following repeat. They never wrap between hall ends. Re-entering dancers use the source do-si-do from their exact 1.8-unit facing-row stations, so no balance gathering or extra travel counts are added.

The compiler discovers a state period and validates actual root positions and facing at closure. For every count from 2 through 12 couples, this produces period `2N` and zero transient. That period is a checked result, not an assumed cache modulus. Every figure join and repeat boundary is tested on actual presented poses as well as formation poses.

## Articulated composition refinements

The isolated `becket-star-figures.ts` contains explicit reusable physical contracts:

1. The lark left allemande finishes its named turn while preparing current-neighbor facing during its final source count. Hand strength declines when the fixed-length arms cannot reach; this is explicitly partial contact, never a full-strength missed handhold.
2. A compact left-hand star gathers toward a 0.63-unit radius, performs a complete negative revolution with the matching left tangent, and releases back to the exact original stations inside eight source counts. The contact graph joins opposite actors using left hands. No progression translation is appended.
3. The new-neighbor do-si-do uses a 0.45-unit lateral lane instead of the ordinary primitive's 0.34-unit lane, preserving the same right-shoulder passage, one full traversal, incoming-facing orientation, eight counts, and identity-returning endpoints.

The diagnostic full three-couple/six-repeat cadence pass sampled all figures at 12 samples/count. Every activity had zero foot-reach correction, with fixed leg and arm lengths and one step per musical count. Full-strength star and allemande wrists were exact to numerical precision. The inherited balance wrist residual was at most 0.00415 units. Partial contacts remain present during approach and release.

Sampled new-body checks found positive separation for the allemande and widened do-si-do. The star had positive separation outside its explicitly disclosed common-hand hub; hand/forearm overlap inside that modeled hub reached about 0.029 units. These are sampled meshes, not continuous collision certificates.

## Important inherited limits

The shared closed swing-frame style still intersects the named other dancer's torso at some source balance-to-swing and circle-to-swing endpoints and during swings. A diagnostic observed roughly 0.147 units of forearm/torso penetration in that inherited style. The independently sampled existing Butter frame at beat 62 has the same 0.1470884773849579-unit minimum to numerical precision, establishing an unchanged shared-frame limitation rather than a new CCW route defect. This is reported separately; it is not used as a permissive clearance threshold for the new Becket paths. Existing small self-forearm intersections also remain.

Consequently, passing topology, cadence and new-path contact checks is not a statement that the whole dance is collision-free, anatomically exact, or physically safe instruction. Public registration must keep that distinction visible. No live browser GPU or human dance-teacher review is claimed by these offline tests.
