## Public handoff

A `Dance.progressionEvents` entry includes:

- `afterFigureId`: exact source figure whose actual endpoint changes the relation
- `count`: declared net progression; the three whole-dance adapters currently accept one
- `waitingRoleArrangement`: `lark-left` or `lark-right`, independent of circulation
- `initialBinding`: optional `retain-starting-neighbors`, consumed only by the separately gated deferred-binding source family
- `source`: `sourceId`, `url`, `clause`, and `basis` (`source-explicit` or `reviewed-interpretation`)

The event dispatcher consumes one declaration. Extra event fields, unsupported counts, multiple declarations, contradictions and missing figure capabilities reject rather than being silently ignored. Compilation owns and freezes source and identity metadata.

`compileProgressingImproper` consumes an actual complete left-star permutation followed by explicitly rebound registered primitive/chain figures. The tail must leave at least eight source beats for end exchange and must finish every round in the exact next-round logical slots. Simplicity Swing is the reviewed complete-source example. `compileHalfHeyProgressingBecket` consumes Pandora's constrained complete figure/role sequence. Neither dispatches by a dance title or source ID. They verify actual root endpoints before rebinding; no progression motion is added by the event itself.

Current explicit waiting groups also produce per-frame, couple-numbered end cues, distinguish conventional and indecent role placement, and identify the next source entry count.

Every figure boundary and repeat boundary is continuous in root position and facing. Finite-state repetition is detected and checked against the actual root endpoint state; the cache does not assume a period of `2 * couples`. Odd lines have explicit end participants. The two-couple Pandora case keeps both couples inactive through the next opening half until its declared re-entry event, avoiding a fictitious Becket formation.

## Reusable topology and composition

`progression-topology.ts` supplies:

- `bindActorGroups`: arbitrary explicitly partitioned active groups, persistent partners, reciprocal named neighbors, and explicit waiting actors
- `rebindAtEndpoint`: epoch-checked identity change only after the producing figure reaches all declared actor endpoints
- `bindDirectedCoupleSlots`: exact integer-rank finite-line binding; no nearest-person search
- `compileSameEndExchange`: a compact split-partner exchange followed by an absolutely stationary wait; useful for Pandora's indecent ends and ordinary improper ends
- `createFiniteBecketState` and `compileFiniteBecketProgression`: independently selectable CW/CCW circulation and lark-left/lark-right placement
- `compileFiniteProgressionSchedule`: multiple separately timed one-place steps and intermediate neighbor epochs, explicit count checking, and source phrase lengths independent of 64
- `bindBecketDiagonal`: exact left/right diagonal groups with explicit unmatched end couples
- `compileSameEndArc`: a scoped physical-end bicycle-chain arc; it cannot connect the top of a hall to its bottom
- `selectProgressionAssertion`: requires explicit source choice when assertions disagree, while retaining every alternative

`event-section.ts` composes existing `MotionPlan` primitives on the source beat grid. A step may declare a checked binding event immediately before it. Inactive tracks name exactly the waiting actors. The section checks entry continuity, finite positions/contacts, sampled global torso clearance between distinct minor sets, and sampled root speed. These section checks supplement primitive certificates; they are not a continuous articulated-body certificate.

## Half-hey timing

Pandora uses all four completed source pass layers `MR;PL;WR;NL`. Its verified corner permutation is a 180-degree diagonal exchange. Logical linear end order and physical corner order are different representations and are tested separately.

The modeled gates are 2.25, 3.75, 5.25 and 6.75 beats into its eight-beat half hey, with slot spacing 1.05 and pass clearance 0.66. This allocation keeps the continuous primitive root speed certificate at or below 1.2 scene units per beat and retains time after the final completed pass for departure facing. It neither truncates the final pass nor appends extra source beats. Gate timing is a reviewed animation interpretation, not an additional source claim.

`withBalanceStance` prepares reachable count-1 handholds in actual presented roots. `formationSample` retains the separately inspectable logical endpoint trajectory. Beat 0 is musical count 1; a balance occupies four beats and immediately hands to its source swing or arch. The gait clock remains one footfall per beat.

## Verified and deliberately not claimed

Focused regression coverage checks complete source counts/credit, public dispatch, source argument consumption, input ownership, neighbor changes, finite-end closure, same-end exchange timing, and root/facing joins for even and odd line lengths.

The generalized finite-shift tests exercise 2–12 couples, both circulations, both role placements, separate double steps, exact diagonals, non-64-beat sections and retained source conflicts. They do not register an arbitrary double-progression or reverse-Becket source dance.

Dreamland Races still has distinct CW registry and CCW author/ContraDB assertions. Pigeon on the Jetway's original and ContraDB B2 variants remain separate. Their metadata and topology tests are not whole-dance execution claims. Pigeon's ricochet/gyre/diagonal choreography, Dreamland's wave/circulate/rollaway choreography, and Anticipation's complete along-line encounter order need their respective motion and source composition gates. The same-end arc alone does not enable Anticipation.

## Prototype body-fidelity evidence

The independent actual-Avatar audit of source 175 and Pandora sampled every actor at 200 samples per beat across two complete rounds. Both retained integer-count landings with no offbeat steps. It measured legacy star reach correction up to 17.8 mm for source 175 and a modeled Pandora half-hey correction up to 70.8 mm. Those are explicit prototype gait-fidelity limitations, not evidence of anatomically exact movement. Source semantics, named interactions, actual finite-end paths and count timing have separate passing checks.

Frederick's reused-figure probe measured zero leg corrections in full first rounds at two and six couples. Its inherited same-role allemande showed a 109.8 mm maximum wrist miss and balance a 4.15 mm miss; these are disclosed contact-fidelity limitations. The event composer does not hide those measurements by weakening contact strength.
