# Strict carried role-turn/swing prototype

`src/role-turn-swing-prototype.ts` is unregistered. It handles only a six-count
robin right allemande three quarters followed by a ten-count neighbor swing,
from actual released standard corners with current neighbors on sidelines.
It consumes carried actor identities, roots, facing, relationships and epoch.
It does not identify dances by title or source ID and does not admit source 110.

The first five counts complete the exact three-quarter root orbit at the existing
.55 held radius. Entry facing reaches the other robin before the hand acquisition
window begins at .85. The old right-hand hold remains full at beat 5. Roots and
facing stay fixed during its release from 5 to 5.25. The final .75 count physically
approaches and turns into a vertical .62 neighbor swing frame. No endpoint reset
or relation rebinding occurs.

Swing-frame acquisition starts no earlier than 5.75 and is additionally gated by
actual shoulder-to-target reach. The shoulder is offset .235 from the body root
at the actual yaw, at height 1.355; all held hand targets, including reciprocal
joined hands and body-target contacts, must be within .64. The gate smoothly
reaches the exact existing consumer contact strengths and points at count 6.
In the baseline geometry positive swing contact begins around 5.858; this late
acquisition is a modeling convention requiring presentation review. Root reach
alone is explicitly not an arm certificate. No arm collision, contact velocity,
GPU, stance-prepared body or anatomical certificate is claimed.

Three prototype tests and two unchanged legacy guard/control tests pass. At
120 Hz, observed root clearance is .606387, maximum root speed 1.399645, maximum
yaw rate 2.617563 and maximum shoulder target reach .639688. Tests prove exact
.75 winding at beat 5, release before travel, exact next-consumer roots/facing/raw
contacts, immutable input capture, translation, nonzero incoming facing, permuted
opaque actor/couple IDs, and strict rejected arguments and stale/held boundaries.

Remaining: independent review of positive-strength contact reach and trajectory,
adjacent groups, repeat/finite-end frames, and the distinct later source-110
rollaway contract. Source import, legacy engine, registry and Ready remain
unchanged. This local prototype is evidence for a candidate shared adapter,
not evidence that source 110 is playable.
