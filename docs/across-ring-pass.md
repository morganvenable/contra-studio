counts, explicitly chosen current-neighbor right shoulders, and the immediate
persistent-partner gyre. It uses the existing measured ring-pass kernel in the
actual neighbor-axis frame. The final 1.5 counts prepare body facing toward the
actual persistent partner while roots finish the declared passage. It never
changes partner, neighbor, or epoch bindings.

pass once at the anatomical right-shoulder gate; persistent partners occupy the
measured paired exit stations. Other successors, unknown hand state, omitted
shoulder/receiver, changed counts, progression at the passage, or unsafe geometry
remain rejected. No fallback to a terminal progression pass is allowed.
