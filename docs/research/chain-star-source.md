# PB&J source discrepancy

Retrieved 2026-10-22: https://www.billolsondance.com/pbj (Bill Olson’s own dance page).

The author describes chains flowing into mirrored hands-across stars: Larks chain before the right star, then Robins chain before the left star. ContraDB 1000’s hook reverses that order. The simulator retains the literal table and original hook under a removable discrepancy interpretation, not a verified hook instruction. No source counts or rows are changed. The author combines A2 into sixteen counts; ContraDB’s six-count circle and ten-count swing remain the retained source allocation.

The author also names the final-star cue to identify the next Lark for a right allemande. The parser accepts that cue only at a marked final star when the next cycle actually begins the matching role/right one-and-a-half allemande. Whole-dance identity and endpoint tests remain required.
