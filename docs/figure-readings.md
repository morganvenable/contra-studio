# Figure readings for owner review

Where ContraDB text is ambiguous, the engine draws the standard contra reading
below. One line each; change any of them by telling us the other reading.

## Heys, stars, petronella, roll away, Rory O'More, passes

- A trailing note that only says whom the dancers meet next or dance with ("to new neighbors", "with shadows", "past neighbors", "(no clap)", "a little more to face partners", footnote marks) is drawn as the figure it names; a note that adds motion ("larks turn back over right shoulder", "end in long waves") leaves the row undrawn.
- Ricochet ("robins ricochet"): the two who would pass in the middle walk in, loop back like the dancers at the ends of the hey, and go back out to their own side; the rest of the hey is unchanged.
- A ricochet with no time named is the named dancers' first meeting in the middle ("robins ricochet to start" is that first meeting too).
- "Until partners meet the second time": the hey goes pass after pass and stops, slowing down, as those two come face to face; it does not finish that pass.
- "Larks drop out" at the end of a star is a note (they let go where the star leaves them).
- "Star left 3/4" is a star of 3 places; "1/2" and "halfway" are 2.
- "Balance & petronella left" moves everyone one place to the left, turning to the left.
- Roll away from a ring ("balance the ring, larks roll away partners"): the rolled dancer crosses in front of the roller, toward the middle, and both end facing the middle.
- "Pull by right" with no one named, or named as shadows: the dancers who face each other pull by.
- "Balance & larks Rory O'More right": only the pairs holding that hand with a lark slide; anyone else stands still.
- Revolving door ("robins take right hands and drop off partners on other side"): the door dancers turn once around by that hand, back to their places; the dancers they carry go halfway and are left on the other side, facing in.
- Cross trails: the first named pair crosses in the first half of the beats, the second pair passes in the second half.
- Arch and dive ("twos arch ones dive"): the two couples trade places straight through each other, the divers drawn close under the arching couple's raised inside hands, and end facing the way they walked (on to the next couple).
- Half figure eight ("ones half figure 8 below"): the robin goes through the middle first unless the row names a leader; each dancer loops behind and around the outside of the standing dancer on the far side and ends in the partner's place, facing across; the other couple stands still.
- A hey "until larks meet" with no time named stops at their first meeting.
- Roll away with shadows: the shadow is the nearest dancer of the other role who is neither partner nor neighbor.
- Zig zag ("zig left zag right"): the two pairs of the minor set (partners unless neighbors are named) trade places as units, stepping to the named side as they pass, everyone keeping their facing (the stand-in for a half poussette).
- A hey on a diagonal runs along the line between the two diagonal couples, as an ordinary hey turned onto that line.
- Star promenade "larks pick up partner" / "scooping your partner" is the ordinary star promenade; an allemande "to pick up partner for a star promenade" is the allemande alone.
- "Balance the star" is drawn as a ring balance (step in toward the middle and back).
- Gate ("larks gate neighbors to face into the set"): each gater pivots in place holding the near hand while the other walks around them, the shorter way round (forward when equal), until both face the named way, arm's length apart; "(cw)"/"(ccw)" notes are not drawn separately.
- "Balance & petronella turning to face partners", "(standard 4 person petronella)", "roll away ... half sashay alone set" (along) and "star ... & look for new neighbor" are read as the plain figure.
- Becket progression when partners stand across the set (after "circle left 3 places" then a zig zag or half poussette): each couple joins the couple beside it along the set that it was not just dancing with; neighbors are the other-role dancers of the two couples.
- A roll away in under four beats: the rolled dancer's spin is reported as styled motion (like the Rory twirl), since a full turn in two beats is faster than an ordinary walk turn.
- Contra corners: the actives turn by the right ¾ (4 beats), each turns the other-role corner at their end of the set by the left once around (4¾), the actives turn by the right ½ (2½), and each turns the other corner by the left once around (4¾), ending back to back in the middle; corners come from the neighboring minor sets too, and step in to arm's length and back.
- Mad ricochet (Don Flaherty): a mad robin whose dancers in front walk all the way into the middle, meet the same role from the other side with both hands, and bounce back; the other role does the same on the way back.
- Star thru: lark's right hand with robin's left, the robin turns a quarter left under the arch and the lark a quarter right; they trade places and end side by side facing the lark's right. "Neighbors California twirl (star through)" from facing is drawn as a star thru.
- Dolphin hey: a hey for three along the set on one figure-eight track, the named couple dancing as one (one close behind the other) and the two others a third of the track apart; the track is turned so the first pass is the one the row names; a full one ends everyone at home. Extra passes ("one extra crossing", "one more time") are separate rows or are left undrawn.
- A short motion after a figure ("; robins turn back over right shoulder", "robins turn around", "larks turn out over left shoulder", "and pass thru to new neighbors") is drawn as its own call over the row's last two beats (a quarter of a shorter row); turning back with no shoulder named goes over the right.
- Facing star ("facing star clockwise 3 places with robins putting their left hands in and backing up"): the named role (the robins when none is named) faces against the way the star turns and backs up, their other hand in the middle.
- Petronella "2 places" / "petronella-nella" / "x2": two places with a turn for each; "1½ places" ends on a diamond with two turns, facing in.
- Star notes read as the plain star: "larks drop out (on home side)", "robins falling in / stepping in behind partners", "a bit more until ...", "just halfway", "with shadow #1", "seek your new neighbor", "to take right hands with next neighbors".
- A trailing turn with no one named ("robins balance & pull by right, turn back in") is done by the dancers the row named; a turn named alongside a pass ("larks pass by ..., robins turn over right shoulders") is drawn right after the pass.
- Hey rows: "pass partner right to start" / "(pull by r)" restate the first pass; "passing by the left" restates the shoulder; blank shoulders ("____ in center") take the row's "passing ... shoulders", else rights; "until partners meet back on home side" is the usual "until partners meet"; "when the robins meet the second time they ricochet" is a ricochet on that meeting; "larks finish an ordinary hey" is a note.
- Zig zag rows saying whom the pairs pass or meet, or who leads out ("larks lead out, robins in to face new couple", "into a ring with new neighbors") read as the plain zig zag; one forming a wave or another figure does not.
- "Ones full figure 8": two half figure eights, around one standing dancer then the other, ending home.
- "Pull by right diagonal" with no diagonal side named: the diagonal on the side of the hand; whoever faces across that diagonal pulls by.
- Contra corners notes "home file", "opposite file", "ending progressed", "(alternate with twos)" read as plain contra corners.
- A petronella over more than one place cuts inside the ring and its extra spins are reported as styled motion (like the Rory twirl).
- Circular hey: around the ring of places like a grand right and left without hands, straight from place to place, the named pair passing first by the named shoulder and the passes alternating; full is four changes, half two.
