# Ordinary full-hey handoffs

## Scope

The ordinary full-hey adapter remains unchanged for terminal current-neighbor
heys. A separate, capability-gated adapter composes a complete sixteen-count,
role-first ordinary full hey from persistent partner sidelines into a partner
balance or swing. It does not progress the dancers. Progression is established
by the complete source sequence's physical endpoint.

Admission requires explicit opposite center/end shoulders, one lark and robin
on each partner sideline, a valid current relationship epoch, sixteen source
counts, and a matching immediately following partner figure. Declared axes,
partial counts, extra hand/turn modifiers and progression on this hey reject.
No dance title or catalog identity selects a path.

The reviewed `ordinaryHeyHandoffs` capability is enabled in registered defaults;
explicit false retains a fail-closed control. The source choices use
`same-slots-partner-balance` or `same-person-swing`.
The former requires a four-count partner balance. The latter requires a partner
swing of at least four counts. The source normalizer additionally requires the
corresponding literal successor clause. Existing terminal, Becket, diagonal,
ricochet and half-hey adapters are preserved.

## Typed boundary

`formation-handoff.ts` records stable actor IDs, roles/couples, reciprocal partner
and neighbor bindings, relationship epoch, source clock, ordered occupied slots,
paired geometry, facing and all held-contact fields. Capturing or comparing a
state rejects nonfinite values, unknown/hidden/accessor declarations, malformed
relations and mismatched geometry. Comparison never moves a root, changes a
person, acquires a hold or adds time.

`ordinary-hey-handoff.ts` retains eight pass layers and twelve literal encounters.
The independent pass graph proves identity permutation before endpoint geometry
is supplied to `compilePartialHey`. Every actor returns to its incoming corner.
Pair gaps become 1.18 for balance or 0.62 for swing. These are uncalibrated model
units. Contact preparation occurs after the final named pass gate, inside the
existing sixteen counts.

The plan's boundary is the primitive/formation boundary. The established stance
wrapper separately acquires the two balance hands during the preceding final
count. The durable source tests also capture and compare the effective wrapped
states, including all contact metadata. No instantaneous contact repair is
permitted at the actual balance or swing boundary.

  guards; state/identity/contact/clock changes rejected.

  figure/repeat boundary, physical single progression, waiting ends, and title
  and ID invariance. Dense root samples use eight samples per beat over each
  complete finite period.

Observed full-sequence root clearance is at least 0.51410 model units; sampled
maximum root speed is at most 1.22289 model units per beat. Maximum sampled
figure/repeat boundary root displacement at ±10⁻⁷ beats is 1.061×10⁻⁷, and facing
change is below 1.179×10⁻⁷ radians. The path solver separately certifies its own
local pass clearance/speed/yaw budgets. Dense full-sequence samples are not a
continuous collision certificate.

Existing full-hey, ordinary half-hey source and axis-rejection suites are included
in the production regression run. No new articulated-limb or physical-teaching
safety claim is made. The separately reviewed partner-first half-hey contract
is documented in `partner-first-half-heys.md`.
