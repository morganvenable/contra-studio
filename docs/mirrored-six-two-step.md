# Measured right-circle / left-passage adapter

## Contract

`compileMirroredSixCountCircleStep(figure, context, incoming)` accepts a six-count,
three-place circle right for all four actors, followed immediately by the
two-count current-neighbor passage below. It consumes the actual released square
roots, sideline persistent partners and across-set current neighbors. The
measured half-width envelope is unchanged: 0.72–0.90 world units.

`compileMirroredTwoCountPassStep(figure, context, incoming)` accepts
`kind: 'pass-through', beats: 2, who: 'neighbor', passingShoulder: 'left',
entry: 'circle-exit', exit: 'progressed-neighbors'`, with no next figure. It
remeasures the released, forward-facing preparation instead of carrying a hidden
continuation token. Both functions return immutable sequence boundaries, measured
geometry, sampled root budgets and explicit left-shoulder gates.

The circle turns literally right by three places in 5.25 counts and uses the
remaining 0.75 count for release and lane preparation. That internal split is a
schematic implementation convention, not an extra source instruction. The pass
owns its separate two source counts. No figure count is changed.

## Reflection and ownership

The implementation delegates to the unchanged measured left-circle/right-pass
solver after reflecting world x and yaw. Contact points, body-local lateral
coordinates and anatomical hand names are reflected consistently. Reflection is
involutive. The solver still derives its local frame from actual partner edges.

Actor IDs, Lark/Robin roles, persistent couples, partner/current-neighbor maps,
source clocks and relationship epochs remain exact. There is no role swap,
source ID, title or whole-prefix recognition. The adapter changes physical
corners only; progression-event validation and relationship rebinding remain with
the finite source-bound sequencer.

Entry holds must already be released. Both source entry and exit boundaries are
owned and validated. Moved roots, stale epochs, incompatible figure qualifiers,
opposite handedness, retained holds, invalid sample clocks and malformed prepared
lanes are rejected rather than repaired.

first integration attempt, the source owner reported an earlier partner
balance/swing boundary in 3310 outside the paired-radial-frame envelope, and a
3241 half-hey exit whose declared neighbor-balance handoff did not fit its next
neighbor gyre/swing. Those prefixes are outside this adapter's ownership and
have not been silently changed to obtain a passing suffix.

3310's raw struck-through attribution and subsequent correction remain present
in the canonical retained fixture. 3241's separate exact authorship/methodology
commentary review is owned by the source review layer. 480 remains excluded
because its “hey-like figure up/down set” prose has unresolved movement scope.

This is a schematic root/contact proof, not a dense body/anatomical safety
certificate. No all-size or full-body sweep is claimed.
